About Me

I work at the intersection of machine learning, mathematics, and applied AI. My background spans deep learning, computer vision, industrial ML, and mathematical research, with a growing focus on manifold learning and geometric views of representation learning and deep networks.

I am particularly interested in research directions that connect geometry, manifold structure, data analysis, and deep learning theory. At the moment, I am especially interested in collaborations and academic opportunities related to the theory of deep learning.

About

I hold a PhD in mathematics and have worked across software engineering, data science, machine learning, and computer vision in both academic and industrial settings. My projects have ranged from medical imaging and industrial inspection to aviation analytics, optimization, and vision systems.

While much of my earlier work focused on applied machine learning and computer vision, my recent research direction has moved more strongly toward manifold learning, data geometry, and the mathematical foundations of deep learning. I am especially interested in how geometric structure can improve our understanding of learned representations, generalization, and data analysis.

Outside research and engineering, I also teach Dominican bachata. Teaching and building dance communities has shaped how I communicate complex material: clearly, patiently, and with strong attention to structure and interaction.

GitHub repository

h-NNE

A hierarchical dimensionality reduction method related in spirit to t-SNE and UMAP, but designed for speed, simplicity, and structured coarse-to-fine exploration.

  • Built around hierarchical structure in the data
  • Supports interpretable zoom-like exploration
  • Useful for visualization and data analysis

Dancing

Alongside research and engineering, I teach Dominican bachata and help build local dance communities. This reflects another side of what I value: teaching, communication, rhythm, culture, and creating spaces where people can learn and connect.

I see this as complementary to my technical profile rather than separate from it. Both research and dance teaching involve careful structure, sensitivity to detail, and the ability to guide others through complex patterns.
